Methodology

How it works

Rigorous content moderation requires grounded explanations, cross-modal attention, and multi-stream attribution.

Read research paper specifications →

Code-mixed language handling

Social conversations across South Asia interleave Hindi (Devanagari) and Latin phonetic transliteration (Hinglish). Our model deploys subword byte-pair embeddings resilient to non-standard phonetic spelling and colloquial slang.

Explore in detector

Negation and counterspeech

Classifiers often flag innocent quotations or anti-hate advocacy that repeat slurs to condemn them. Bidirectional attention layers assign strong mitigating saliency to condemnatory verbs like "unacceptable" or "completely false."

Test counterspeech example

OCR + multimodal fusion

Memes juxtapose harmless background imagery with toxic typography. Spatial OCR extracts typographic coordinates while intermediate CNN feature representations project visual embeddings into cross-attention transformer heads.

Inspect meme cross-attention

Token attribution

Rather than relying on ungrounded post-hoc approximations, we compute perturbation saliency by observing probability drops under token masking, verified against Area Over the Perturbation Curve (AOPC) standards.

View token heatmaps

Multi-stream Shapley analysis

Exact 3-stream Shapley values decompose decisions into linguistic content, visual typography, and conversational thread context, guaranteeing mathematical efficiency without arbitrary heuristics.

Explore stream decomposition

Fairness audit

We evaluate disparate error rates across dialect groups to ensure false-positive parity. The architecture maintains equalized odds across standard English, Devanagari Hindi, and code-mixed Hinglish subsets.

Models should operate as auditor assistance tools rather than autonomous unreviewed enforcement engines.
Frequently Asked Questions

Technical Clarifications

The detector is intentionally limited to explicit linguistic markers and spatial meme typography. It cannot reliably detect subtle sarcasm devoid of lexical indicators, nor can it reconstruct intentional audio/video deepfakes or resolve long multi-turn threads exceeding several thousand tokens. Furthermore, images lacking legible typographic text are classified on visual semantics alone, which carries higher epistemic uncertainty.
Reported metrics are computed on a strictly stratified 190-sample holdout test partition that was completely isolated from training. Macro-F1 averages the unweighted per-class harmonic mean across both Hateful and Non-Hateful subsets, preventing majority-class distortion. AOPC faithfulness is calculated by progressively ablating top-attributed tokens and verifying corresponding drops in classification confidence.
Standalone classification frequently misidentifies educational discourse, journalism, and counterspeech as hateful because those posts cite offensive phrases verbatim. When conversational context is enabled, the context processing module evaluates the surrounding dialogue intention, dampening false alarms when offensive terms are quoted in condemnation.
Hostile attributions (+) are tokens whose presence significantly increases the model's confidence toward the Hateful label (e.g. slurs, dehumanizing analogies). Mitigating attributions (−) are civil markers, inclusive vocabulary, or condemnatory phrasing whose presence reduces hostility and steers the classification toward Non-Hateful.
