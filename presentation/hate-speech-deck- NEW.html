Introduction

Hate spreads fast online. Most detectors can’t tell you why they flagged a post.

We built a system that detects hate speech, explains its decision word by word, and keeps learning from human feedback.

Our Solution

Detect. Explain. Improve.

01

Multilingual Transformer

BiLSTM + multi‑head attention with char n‑grams. Handles Devanagari and Hinglish natively.

02

Multimodal OCR fusion

EasyOCR reads text inside memes. Cross‑attention joins it with image features.

03

Faithful XAI

Leave‑one‑out token scores, checked with AOPC. Every verdict comes with evidence.

04

Reward + DPO

Human corrections become preference pairs. Regressing models never go live.

Methodology

Six steps, one loop.

1 · Input

Text, image, conversation context

2 · Preprocess

NFKC normalize, script detect, EasyOCR, resize 224×224

3 · Encode

BiLSTM + attention, TF‑IDF baseline, CNN for images

4 · Fuse

Cross‑attention with sigmoid gate

5 · Predict

Guardrail, then label + confidence

6 · Explain

Leave‑one‑out scores, AOPC faithfulness

Feedback → Reward → DPO → new checkpoint → tested against a held‑out set before deploy
